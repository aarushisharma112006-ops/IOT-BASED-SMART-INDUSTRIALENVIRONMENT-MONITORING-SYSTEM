
## IoT-Based Smart Industrial Environment Monitoring System

### Project Overview

The **IoT-Based Smart Industrial Environment Monitoring System** is an Industry 4.0 safety solution engineered to replace traditional, reactive threshold alarms with proactive, predictive environmental hazard tracking. The system continuously samples atmospheric parameters, triggers immediate local alarms during unsafe conditions, uploads telemetry to cloud infrastructure, and utilizes machine learning models to forecast hazards such as toxic gas accumulation and machine overheating.

---

### Key Objectives

* **Continuous Atmospheric Tracking:** Monitor toxic gases/air quality, temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure in real time.


* **Instant On-Site Alerts:** Activate localized audiovisual alarms (buzzer and LED) and OLED displays when thresholds are breached.


* **Cloud Telemetry:** Transmit live data to platforms such as ThingSpeak or Firebase for remote dashboards.


* **Predictive AI Modeling:** Apply a Python-based Random Forest Classifier to detect hazard trends before safety thresholds are crossed.


* **Cost-Effective Scalability:** Implement an automated, low-cost (approx. ₹1,005–₹1,430) hardware architecture.



---

### System Architecture & Hardware Stack

* **Microcontroller:** ESP32 Dev Board (dual-core processing and built-in Wi-Fi communication).


* **Sensors:**
* **MQ-135:** Detects air quality, smoke, and toxic gas concentrations.


* **DHT11:** Measures ambient workspace temperature and relative humidity.


* **BMP280:** Tracks barometric pressure and altitude changes.


* **Raindrop Sensor:** Identifies outdoor moisture or rain events.




* **Outputs & Indicators:** 0.96" I2C OLED display, 5V active buzzer, and warning LED.


* **Software Tools:** Arduino IDE, ThingSpeak / Firebase, Python, and Streamlit.



---

### Working Methodology

1. **Data Acquisition:** The ESP32 collects analog and digital metrics across all attached sensors.


2. **Local Evaluation & Safety Checks:** The controller checks readings against safety limits (e.g., Gas > 400 ppm, Temperature > 45.0°C).


3. **Local Alarm Response:** If limits are breached, GPIO pins trigger the buzzer and LED while printing status updates to the OLED screen.


4. **Cloud Transmission & AI Forecasting:** Data packets are streamed over Wi-Fi every 10 seconds to ThingSpeak and routed to a Streamlit web interface running a Random Forest Classifier to assess and predict hazard status.



---

### Industrial Applications

* **Chemical Manufacturing Plants:** Detecting hazardous gas leaks and preventing chemical poisoning.


* **Smart Warehousing:** Regulating temperature and moisture levels for sensitive goods.


* **Industry 4.0 Facilities:** Preventing machine overheating through ambient temperature anomaly tracking.


* **Underground Mines:** Monitoring pressure drops and gas build-up to maintain worker safety.



---

### Future Scope

* **Edge AI (TinyML):** Embedding the machine learning classifier directly onto the ESP32 microcontroller to enable offline predictions without cloud latency.


* **Automated Ventilation Control:** Interfacing high-power relay modules to switch on industrial exhaust fans upon detecting gas build-up.


* **Expanded Sensor Array:** Adding particulate matter (PM2.5), carbon monoxide (CO), and flame detection modules.


* **Direct Notifications:** Linking the dashboard to Telegram or custom Android alert pipelines.
